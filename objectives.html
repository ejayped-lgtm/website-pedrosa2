What Needs to Change?

Advocacy Goal: To embed climate resilience and sustainability as core, non-negotiable standards in all national and local infrastructure projects.

Key Isues To Address:

Outdated Design Codes: Existing codes may not fully account for future climate projections (e.g., 100-year flood levels are no longer enough).
Lack of Life-Cycle Costing: Focusing only on initial construction cost often neglects the high maintenance and repair costs after disasters.
Poor Materials & Quality: The need for stricter quality control and use of high-performance, durable, and sustainable construction materials.
