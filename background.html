The Philippine Context: A Vulnerable Nation

The Urgency: Why Resilient Infrastructure is Non-Negotiable

High Disaster Risk: The Philippines is in the Pacific Ring of Fire (earthquakes/volcanoes) and the Typhoon Belt (3rd most disaster-prone country globally).
Economic Impact: Disasters cause billions in damage annually, wiping out years of infrastructure investment and hindering economic growth.
Climate Change Threat: Rising sea levels, more intense rainfall, and stronger typhoons are accelerating infrastructure degradation.
