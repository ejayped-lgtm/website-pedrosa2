Who is Responsible?

A Collaborative Effort: Investing in Our Future

We call on:

Government/Policy Makers (DPWH, LGUs): Mandate the use of Climate Risk Assessments for all new and existing infrastructure proposals. Prioritize long-term resilience funding.
Civil Engineers (PICE): Champion Continuing Professional Development (CPD) on climate-resilient design, BIM (Building Information Modeling) for better planning, and new technologies. Uphold the highest ethical and quality standards in all projects.
The Public: Support policies that allocate resources to resilient infrastructure, recognizing it as an investment in public safety and economic stability.
